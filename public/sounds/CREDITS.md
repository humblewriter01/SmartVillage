# Animal sound recordings

These short recordings are bundled for offline playback. The original recordings
and the edited versions listed here are CC0 1.0 or public domain; credits are
retained to document provenance. No synthesized or spoken animal imitations are
used. The intermediate projects trimmed, normalized and/or converted the files to
MP3; SmartVillage uses those files without further edits.
