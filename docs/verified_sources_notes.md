# Verified source notes

## Nigerian crops

FAO’s Nigeria at a Glance page identifies maize, cassava, sorghum, cowpea, rice, yam, and millet as major Nigerian staple crops: https://www.fao.org/nigeria/fao-in-nigeria/nigeria-at-a-glance/en/

## Malaria safety guidance

WHO states that mild malaria symptoms may include fever, chills, and headache, while severe symptoms can include extreme fatigue, impaired consciousness, convulsions, difficulty breathing, dark or bloody urine, jaundice, and abnormal bleeding. WHO advises emergency care for severe symptoms and notes that early treatment can prevent progression: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/malaria

These notes support offline educational guidance only. They do not establish a clinical diagnostic model or authorize treatment recommendations.
